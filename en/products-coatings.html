Coating Products

Industrial Protective & Decorative Coatings

We distribute Taiho, PETERS LI, Humiseal and other coating brands, covering interior decorative coatings, industrial protective coatings, and electronic moisture-proof insulation coatings for interior decoration, equipment protection, and electronic component moisture protection.

These products comply with material standards for home appliance manufacturing, electronics, and telecommunications equipment, combining environmental responsibility with durability. We also provide product selection guidance to help clients find the optimal coating solution.
