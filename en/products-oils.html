Oils & Lubricants

Industrial Lubrication & Equipment Maintenance

We distribute Kyodo Yushi, Fuchs, Feitian and other lubricant brands, covering industrial greases, gearbox oils, and specialty lubricants for mechanical equipment lubrication, power system maintenance, and component protection.

These products comply with equipment operation and maintenance standards for automotive, telecommunications, and new energy industries, delivering stable lubricity and durability. We also provide product selection and application guidance to help clients maintain operational efficiency and extend equipment service life.
