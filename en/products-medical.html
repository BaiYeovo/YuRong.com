Medical Products

Medical Testing Equipment & Consumables

We offer medical products across two categories: testing equipment — hemoglobin analyzers, spectrophotometers, and fully automatic chemical analyzers for clinical diagnostics; and medical consumables — oxygen therapy devices, breathing circuits, and surgical tubing for therapeutic support.

These products comply with clinical application standards, combining regulatory compliance with proven reliability. We also provide usage guidance to help healthcare institutions enhance the efficiency and safety of diagnostic and treatment workflows.
