Two Decades of Professional Distribution
Deep Industry Expertise

Yu Rong Mechanical and Electrical (Dalian) Co., Ltd. was established in 2007, headquartered in the coastal city of Dalian, Liaoning Province.

Our Dalian HQ is staffed with 6 professional team members, with 1 dedicated specialist each in our East China and South China offices, forming a comprehensive service network covering key regions.

As a comprehensive private enterprise focused on the distribution and technical services of chemicals (including hazardous chemicals), our core mission is to "deliver durable and reliable industrial-grade solutions." Our product portfolio spans adhesives, coatings, oils, magnetic materials, medical, energy-saving, resins, and more. Through high-quality customized solutions, we deeply serve clients across semiconductor packaging, electronics, medical, home appliances, telecommunications, automotive, textiles, new energy, and other full-industry-chain sectors.

Since our founding, our team has been deeply engaged in understanding industry needs and building a trusted product and technical service system. Looking ahead, we will continue leveraging our professional expertise to provide precise and efficient chemical application solutions for clients across more sectors, empowering industrial upgrades and sustainable development.
