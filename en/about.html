Company Profile

Established in 2007
Deep Industry Expertise

Established in 2007 and headquartered in the coastal city of Dalian, Liaoning Province.

Our Dalian HQ is staffed with 6 professionals, with 1 dedicated specialist each in our East China and South China offices, forming a comprehensive service network across key regions.

As a comprehensive private enterprise focused on chemical (including hazardous chemical) distribution and technical services, our core mission is to "deliver durable, reliable industrial-grade solutions." Our product portfolio spans adhesives, coatings, oils, magnetic materials, medical, energy-saving, resins, and more. Through high-quality customized solutions, we serve clients across semiconductor packaging, electronics, medical, home appliances, telecommunications, automotive, textiles, new energy, and other industries.

Since our founding, our team has been deeply engaged in understanding industry needs and building a trusted product and technical service system. Looking ahead, we will continue leveraging our expertise to provide precise, efficient chemical application solutions for clients across more sectors, empowering industrial upgrades and sustainable development.
