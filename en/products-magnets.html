Magnetic Materials

High-Performance Magnetic Materials

We provide magnetic ring-related basic materials including rare earth magnetic powders and granular materials. These materials feature stable magnetic properties and serve as core ingredients for electromagnetic components and magnetic devices.

These products serve the manufacturing needs of the electronics and telecommunications equipment industries. We also provide material selection guidance to help clients find the optimal magnetic material solution.
