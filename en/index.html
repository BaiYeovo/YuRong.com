About Us

Two Decades of Professional Distribution
Deep Industry Expertise

Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Dalian, Liaoning Province, our team spans Dalian HQ and East/South China offices. We are a comprehensive private enterprise specializing in the distribution and technical service of chemicals (including hazardous chemicals).

Our multi-category portfolio covers adhesives, coatings, oils, medical products, and more — delivering tailored material solutions for clients across semiconductor packaging, electronics, medical, home appliances, automotive, and other industries.

Our Products

Adhesives

Distributing Shin-Etsu, LOCTITE and other leading brands for sealing, bonding, and thermal management in semiconductor packaging and electronics.

Coatings

Distributing Taiho, Humiseal and other brands for decorative, industrial protective, and electronic moisture-proof coating applications.
