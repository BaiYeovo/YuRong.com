Medical Products

Medical Testing Equipment & Consumables

We offer medical products covering testing equipment and consumables. Testing equipment includes hemoglobin analyzers, spectrophotometers, and fully automatic chemical analyzers (for clinical indicator testing). Medical consumables include oxygen therapy devices, breathing circuits, and surgical tubing (for therapeutic support applications).

These products meet clinical application standards in the medical industry, combining compliance with reliability. We also provide usage guidance and other services to help healthcare institutions enhance the efficiency and safety of their diagnostic and treatment workflows.

Energy-Saving Products

Efficient Energy-Saving Lighting Solutions

We provide a diverse range of energy-saving lighting products, including tubes (T8/T5), linear lights, panel lights, downlights, ceiling lights, and emergency tubes. Our lineup features CSS/CS5 energy-saving tubes, MSBT linear lights, and other models with Mesh and PLC material options.

These products feature low energy consumption and high brightness — core energy-saving characteristics suitable for office, commercial, and residential lighting applications, helping users achieve energy-efficient lighting upgrades and improved luminous environments.

Magnetic Materials
