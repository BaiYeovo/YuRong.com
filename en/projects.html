Our Innovation

Industry-Focused Product Technology

Scale Inhibition Technology

CaCO₃ (Calcite hard scale) + CO₂ + H₂O → Ca(HCO₃)₂
Ca(HCO₃)₂ → CaCO₃ (Aragonite soft scale) + CO₂ + H₂O

Under magnetic field influence, water solubility increases, viscosity decreases, and permeability enhances — allowing water molecules to more easily penetrate micro-gaps in existing calcite scale. The magnetized water molecules alter and disrupt the crystalline structure of hard calcium carbonate scale, causing old scale to be penetrated, cracked, peeled, and detached from equipment surfaces.

Honeycomb Catalysis Technology

  • Improved discharge compliance, reduced wastewater treatment costs
