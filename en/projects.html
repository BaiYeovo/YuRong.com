Engineering Adhesive Industry Solutions

We have accumulated extensive application experience across automotive interiors, electronics & semiconductors, new energy, furniture & building materials, and more — delivering professional adhesive solutions for our clients

Automotive Interior

Automotive Interior Component Bonding Application

Provided water-based polyurethane (YR9688) and hot-melt PUR (YR9860) combined solutions for headliner lamination, seat foam bonding, and door panel wrapping, delivering high initial tack, low odor, and excellent heat resistance while meeting automotive VOC emission standards.

Electronics & Semiconductors

Semiconductor Packaging Thermal Potting

Provided Shin-Etsu thermal conductive adhesive and LOCTITE potting compound for semiconductor chip packaging and electronic component fixation & heat dissipation, delivering excellent thermal conductivity and electrical insulation to enhance product reliability.

New Energy

Power Battery Module Bonding & Sealing

Provided structural adhesive and sealant solutions for power battery module fixation and battery pack waterproof sealing, delivering excellent vibration resistance and high/low temperature performance to ensure safe battery system operation.

Furniture & Building Materials

Custom Furniture Edge Banding & Wrapping

Provided hot-melt edge banding (YR5362) and 3D wrapping adhesive solutions for cabinet door panels, wardrobe panels, and other custom furniture processes, offering high initial tack, heat resistance, environmental friendliness, and low odor.

Textile & Fabrics

Garment Fabric Lamination Bonding

Provided water-based polyurethane and hot-melt film solutions for garment fabric lamination, automotive interior fabric bonding, and footwear material bonding, offering excellent softness, wash resistance, and high bond strength for diverse textile industry needs.
